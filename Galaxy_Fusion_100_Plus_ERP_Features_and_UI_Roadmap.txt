SECTION 1: AUDIT & ANALYSIS OF THE CURRENT GALAXY FUSION PROJECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Current Codebase & Architecture Overview
- Multi-Tenant College Scoping (`src/types/index.ts`, `src/data/mockCampusData.ts`, `schema.sql`):
  The app currently supports individual college workspaces (such as GLA University Mathura, IET Lucknow, KIET Ghaziabad, AKGEC Ghaziabad, and JSS Noida) with `colleges` and `college_id` tenancy across 13 Supabase PostgreSQL tables (`profiles`, `projects`, `groups`, `group_members`, `group_join_requests`, `group_messages`, `teammate_requests`, `faculty_projects`, `jobs`, `materials`, `suggestions`, `feedback`, and `notifications`).
- Five-Tab React Native Architecture (`App.tsx`):
  1) `FeedScreen.tsx` (Campus Hub): 3-Way Collaboration Banner (`Student–Student`, `Student–Faculty`, `Student–College`), "Ask for Teammates" composer, 8-module launcher grid, and feed cards with embedded GitHub repository cards.
  2) `TeamsScreen.tsx` (Teammates): 3 sub-tabs (`🤝 Ask Teammates`, `🔍 Find Peers`, `💬 Skill Groups`) enabling students to broadcast missing hackathon skills, search verified college peers by skill/branch, and chat in real time.
  3) `ReposScreen.tsx` (GitHub): Campus repository explorer with language filters (`TypeScript`, `React Native`, `Python`, `C++`), star counters, and direct GitHub code links.
  4) `CareerScreen.tsx` (College & Labs): 4 sub-tabs (`🏛️ College Openings & T&P`, `🔬 Faculty Research Labs`, `📣 College Townhall`, `🎓 Mentors & Alumni`) with Semester 1–8 CGPA calculation and auto-eligibility checks.
  5) `ProfileScreen.tsx` (Profile): Unified LinkedIn + GitHub student portfolio with a 12-week commit heatmap, skill endorsements, certifications, study materials, campus clubs, and anonymous feedback.

1.2 Key Strengths & Current Gaps Identified in the Audit
- Strengths:
  * Combines verified college roll-number identity with real proof-of-work (GitHub repositories) and career readiness (Semester 1–8 CGPA + T&P placement drives).
  * Bridges the three core campus relationships (`Student–Student`, `Student–Faculty`, `Student–College`) in a single React Native codebase that also runs in the browser (`index.html` + `main.js`).
- Current Functional & UI Gaps Compared to Full-Scale College ERP Portals:
  * Missing daily high-frequency ERP utility hooks (live QR attendance marking, daily class timetable with room numbers, exam hall-ticket downloads, fee/dues status, hostel/gate-pass workflows, and academic assignment submissions) that bring students into the app 3–5 times every day.
  * Information density on `FeedScreen.tsx` and `ProfileScreen.tsx`: the 8-module launcher grid, role switcher bar, and multi-tab sub-navigation compete visually and need progressive disclosure, Bento-grid hierarchy, and native gesture sheets.
  * GitHub data (`ReposScreen.tsx`) and academic records (`CareerScreen.tsx`) currently rely on manual text entry rather than live GitHub REST/GraphQL OAuth syncing and verified registrar/ERP grade locks.

SECTION 2: RESEARCH ON LEADING COLLEGE ERP PORTALS & STUDENT APPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To design the next-generation roadmap for Galaxy Fusion, we benchmarked 12 leading Indian and global higher-education ERP portals, campus recruitment platforms, and student builder networks:

1. Digii / CollPoll ([Digii (formerly CollPoll) Product Profile](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/collpoll) & [CollPoll Smart Attendance System](https://digiicampus.com/blog/maximize-academic-efficiency-with-collpolls-attendance-management-system/)):
   - Core ERP & Engagement Modules: Offers 40+ integrated solutions across Administration (admission automation, fee management, infrastructure & venue booking, hostel management, mess/canteen automation, placement & internship), Learning (choice-based course registration, timetable management, smart attendance, outcome-based education, examination management), and Campus Experience (clubs/chapters, campus commerce, 1,000+ dynamic workflow automations, and student e-portfolios).
   - Key Lesson for Galaxy Fusion: Students use a campus app daily when administrative workflows (venue booking, gate passes, attendance, mess/canteen QR) live alongside social clubs and e-portfolios.

2. Superset ([Superset Campus Placement Automation](https://joinsuperset.com/blogs/from-overwhelmed-to-empowered-why-universities-need-campus-placement-automation-software-now/) & [Superset Faculty Placement Alignment](https://joinsuperset.com/blogs/faculty-as-placement-champions-enabling-academic-industry-alignment-through-placement-software/)):
   - Core Placement Modules: Centralizes placement officers, students, and recruiters with automated eligibility filtering, AI-powered resume parsing & shortlisting, calendar-synced interview slot scheduling, live virtual drive-day control centers, offer letter tracking, and NAAC accreditation reporting.
   - Key Lesson for Galaxy Fusion: Upgrade our `CareerScreen.tsx` placement portal from simple "1-Tap Apply" cards into a multi-stage application pipeline (`Applied -> Aptitude Test -> Technical Round -> HR -> Offer Letter`) with automated batch/branch/CGPA/backlog rule enforcement.

3. Camu Digital Campus ([Camu University Management ERP](https://camudigitalcampus.com/university-management-erp-software/) & [Camu Student Information System](https://camudigitalcampus.com/student-information-system/)):

   - Key Lesson for Galaxy Fusion: Connect student projects and study materials (`ProfileScreen.tsx`) directly to course codes and CBCS credits so academic coursework and GitHub proof-of-work reinforce each other.

4. Academia ERP by Serosoft ([Academia Student Portal & App](https://www.academiaerp.com/modules/student-portal-and-app/) & [Academia Mobile SIS & SERA AI](https://www.academiaerp.com/blog/mobile-access-in-student-information-systems-key-features-and-benefits/)):

   - Key Lesson for Galaxy Fusion: Formalize our `Student–Faculty` research lab slots with a structured Research Project & Grants Management workflow and upgrade our suggestion box into a tracked Grievance & Townhall Service Desk with SLA timers.

6. MasterSoft Cloud ERP ([MasterSoft Student Information Management System](https://www.mastersoft.ai/student-information-management-system-sims) & [MasterSoft SIS Gartner Overview](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/mastersoft-student-information-system)):
